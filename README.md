# GapLogic

GapLogic is an automated competitive intelligence engine designed to turn market landscape data into an aggressive 30-day "Overtake Strategy." 

Instead of generic research, this tool maps your brand against hundreds of competitors in real-time, filters for the most direct threats, and produces a tactical report detailing where competitors are vulnerable and where your own brand has blind spots.

## Key Capabilities

- **Automated Market Sweep:** Automatically scans up to 100 competitors in your niche to identify the real threats.
- **Deep-Dive Gap Analysis:** Performs an aggressive strategic analysis of your top competitors to uncover their unique advantages, vulnerabilities, and strategic errors.
- **Actionable Overtake Playbook:** Generates a specific, actionable 30-day plan to steal market share and patch your own operational blind spots.

## How It Works

1. **Baseline Setup:** Analyzes your client's current brand positioning and target audience.
2. **Market Discovery:** Uses real-time search APIs to find the current competitive landscape.
3. **Filtering:** Applies an AI-driven "light scan" to filter 100+ competitors down to the top 5 strategic threats.
4. **War Room Analysis:** Generates a detailed "Overtake Report" containing:
    - Competitor vulnerabilities.
    - Tactical maneuvers for immediate implementation.
    - Long-term defensive strategies to protect market share.

## Usage

1. Configure your client's URL and target audience in `config.json`.
2. Run `Run_Lead_Bot.bat`.
3. The tool generates a `GAPLOGIC_REPORT.md` and opens it automatically upon completion.
